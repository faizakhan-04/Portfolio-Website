How I think

→

I start with observation

I notice what people do, what they avoid, where they hesitate, and what feels easy or difficult.

↻

I look for patterns

I connect observations, behaviours, needs and evidence to understand what is really going on.

◎

I connect the system

I look beyond the individual touchpoint into the people, processes, platforms and handoffs that shape the experience.

✦

I turn mess into direction

I translate insights into clear opportunities, recommendations and next steps that teams can act on.
