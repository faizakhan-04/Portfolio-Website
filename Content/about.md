## Main bio (from Notion — use this exact text)

I've always been drawn to the small things; the 
decisions people make, the habits they fall into, 
and the little gaps between how things are designed 
and how they actually work.

That's usually where my work begins.

I work at the intersection of people and product, 
trying to understand the gap between what users 
experience and what teams assume they experience. 
My background in product design and two years of 
teaching have shaped how I ask questions, listen, 
and translate what I find into something teams can 
actually use.

Based in Dubai. Published at ServDes'25, IIT Hyderabad.

Currently open to Service Design and UX Research 
opportunities.

## Journey section (from Notion About Me page)

I didn't take a straight path into UX Research — 
and honestly, I don't think I wanted to.

I come from a product design background, and over 
time I found myself more interested in the *why* 
behind design decisions than the outcomes themselves. 
I enjoyed shaping experiences, but I was always more 
curious about the deeper patterns in how people 
think and behave.

I later spent two years teaching design, which turned 
out to be one of the most formative things I've done. 
It pushed me to break complex ideas into something 
others could actually feel and engage with, and to 
ask much better questions. That's carried directly 
into how I approach research today.

## How I think — four cards

Card 1:
  Icon: →
  Title: I start with observation
  Body: Before questions, I watch. The gap between 
        stated and actual behaviour lives in the 
        quiet moments — the workarounds, the 
        hesitations, the things people stop noticing.

Card 2:
  Icon: ↻
  Title: I look for patterns
  Body: Single behaviours are anecdotes. Repeated 
        across contexts, they become signals. I look 
        for the pressures and beliefs that make 
        people act the way they do.

Card 3:
  Icon: ◎
  Title: I care about systems
  Body: Not just the screen in front of someone — 
        the ecosystem of pressures, actors, and 
        constraints behind it.

Card 4:
  Icon: ✦
  Title: I translate mess into direction
  Body: Research only matters if it moves something. 
        I synthesise findings into clear, honest, 
        directional knowledge that teams can act on.

## Beyond work section (from Notion)

Lately, I've been navigating a new phase of life 
after moving to Dubai; new country, new routines, 
and a lot of internal change.

In the middle of all of that, I've found myself 
returning to something I've always loved; sitting 
in cafés with my cold coffee and laptop, observing 
people, and working quietly on things that matter 
to me.

It reminds me why I'm drawn to this field in the 
first place; to slow down, pay attention, and make 
sense of the world through people.
