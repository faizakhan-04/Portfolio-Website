## Approach section — "How I think" (four cards)

Card 1:
  Title: I start with observation
  Body: I notice what people do, what they avoid, where they
        hesitate, and what feels easy or difficult.

Card 2:
  Title: I look for patterns
  Body: I connect observations, behaviours, needs and evidence
        to understand what is really going on.

Card 3:
  Title: I connect the system
  Body: I look beyond the individual touchpoint into the people,
        processes, platforms and handoffs that shape the
        experience.

Card 4:
  Title: I turn mess into direction
  Body: I translate insights into clear opportunities,
        recommendations and next steps that teams can act on.

Footnote: In practice — semi-structured interviews, service
blueprints, affinity mapping, and concept testing; most recently
across 18 stakeholder interviews with 5 NGOs for StraySafe.
